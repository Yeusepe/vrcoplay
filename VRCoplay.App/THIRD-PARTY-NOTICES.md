## GStreamer

- Native runtime: [GStreamer 1.28.7](https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/releases/1.28/).
- Managed bindings: [GstSharpBundle 1.24.3-preview6](https://github.com/aligungr/GstSharpBundle/tree/6f337462422ca11c3ead7d1263b5ff3455f1259a), LGPL-2.1.
- Core/plugins: LGPL-2.1-or-later; the x264 plugin and x264 use GPL-2.0-or-later.
- Exact official wheel URLs, archive checksums and per-file checksums: ThirdParty/GStreamer/runtime.json.
- Dependency versions, build recipes, source requirements and licenses: ThirdParty/GStreamer/README.md and its license texts.
- GPU driver runtimes and Windows graphics APIs are supplied by the operating system/driver installation.

GStreamer replaces the FFmpeg capture and publishing processes. FFmpeg remains a
development receiver in the source tree and is excluded from the distributed app.
